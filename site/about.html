About South Bangla Polli Sangstha

South Bangla Polli Sangstha (SBPS) is a community-focused non-governmental microfinance and rural development organization established on 1 January 2013.

Our organization

Based in Newargachha, Ullapara, Sirajganj, Bangladesh, SBPS works to improve the socioeconomic conditions of underserved communities. Our approach emphasizes inclusive financial services, savings, livelihood opportunities and community participation.

Vision

A poverty-free, economically empowered and socially inclusive society.

Mission

To empower underserved communities through accessible finance, savings and sustainable rural development.

Established

1 January 2013

Our values

  • Integrity, accountability and transparency
  • Respect, inclusion and equal opportunity
  • Responsible financial services and community-led progress
