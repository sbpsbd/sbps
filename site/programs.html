What we do

Our Programs and Activities

SBPS supports rural communities through financial inclusion, savings, women's economic participation and livelihood development.

Microfinance

Financial services intended to support eligible members' livelihoods and responsible income-generating activities.

Savings

Promoting regular savings habits and household financial resilience.

Women's empowerment

Expanding rural women's participation in group-based and economic activities.

Livelihood and enterprise

Encouraging self-employment, microenterprise and sustainable rural livelihoods.

Program eligibility, terms, rates and availability are subject to SBPS policies and applicable regulatory requirements. Contact the office for current details.
