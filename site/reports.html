Audited programmeMicro-Credit Program: Rural Microcredit (RMC) and Micro Enterprise (MEL)

The FY 2025–2026 Auditor's Report and Financial Statements cover SBPS's Microcredit Program for the year ended 30 June 2026. The report identifies JKMNS, Chartered Accountants as the current-year statutory auditor. It includes the statement of financial position, income statement, receipts and payments, cash flows, changes in equity, notes, loan classification and loan-loss-provision schedules, budget analysis and regulatory compliance information.

The complete audit PDF is not posted here because the supplied copy contains confidential operational details, including bank account information and personal particulars. A redacted, formally approved public copy can be published after review. To request the report or an approved public extract, contact the SBPS office.
