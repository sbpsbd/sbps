Who we are

Rooted in community.
Focused on opportunity.

South Bangla Polli Sangstha (SBPS) is a non-governmental microfinance and rural development organization based in Newargachha, Ullapara, Sirajganj, Bangladesh. Established on 2015, SBPS works to improve the socioeconomic conditions of underserved communities.

Our vision

A poverty-free, economically empowered and socially inclusive society where everyone has the opportunity to achieve a better quality of life.

↗

Our mission

To empower underserved communities through accessible financial services, savings, income-generating opportunities, women's economic empowerment and sustainable rural development.
